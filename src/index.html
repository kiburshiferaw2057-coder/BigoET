Game Rules & Steps

  • Classic 75 Line: Complete any full row, column, or diagonal (center FREE space counts as marked).
  • Four Corners: Mark all four outer corner cells.
  • X Pattern: Mark both main diagonals forming an X.
  • Full House: Mark all 24 numbered cells plus the FREE center.

Round Steps (Bingo 1020 v3.0)

  1. Choose language (English, Amharic, Afan Oromo, or Tigrigna) and select a game mode.
  2. Pick a card number from 1 to 600 and monitor the 120-second round timer.
  3. Mark matching called numbers (1–75) on your 5×5 card.
  4. Press Claim Bingo when your selected pattern is completed.
